Projects

Louvre Before You GoFall 2026

Solo project · Web app on GitHub Pages · leslylou0457.github.io/louvre-before-you-go

  • Designed and shipped a mobile-first web guide that prepares first-time visitors for the Louvre: an interactive three-wing map, a 15-chapter catalog of 100 works, and story-based quiz levels.
  • Wrote 28 playable levels in English, each backed by 5–20 cited sources, mostly official Louvre records; wrong answers explain why the mistake is common and how we know the facts.
  • Built it with AI coding tools, with no prior programming background, working from a written product spec; progress saves in the browser with no sign-up.

这一段突出了什么

  • 第一行就说清：做了什么、给谁用、现在能不能打开。
  • 数字都能在网站上数出来：100 件、15 章、28 关。
  • 写了怎么保证内容靠谱，面试被追问时有话可讲。

Projects

Healthcare DecoderFall 2026

Solo project · AI skill for Codex and Claude · Chinese and English versions

  • Built an AI skill that explains healthcare news (licensing deals, drug approvals, policy) to readers without a science background, in a fixed five-part structure that starts from business concepts they already know.
  • Analyzed my own ChatGPT history to find where understanding broke down, and turned it into a learner profile the skill updates after each read.
  • Added a consulting lens to every output (hypothetical client, trade-offs, missing evidence, metrics) and a one-topic infographic; tested on 5 real news events across deals, approvals, policy and medical AI.

这一段突出了什么

  • 没有网页也能写。skill 是能装、能用的东西，一样算作品。
  • 把她的专业（商科）和想去的方向（药企 BD、咨询）连在了一起。
  • 「咨询视角」是她自己加的，写出来就是她的判断。

Buy Now, or Wait?

数据分析 + 网页 · 真实票价数据

大二 · 数学

它是怎么做出来的
  1. 第一版是他自己做出来的，用的是一份公开的 2022 年历史票价数据。
  2. 现在是 0.5 版：三页网页，针对 JFK–LAX 一条航线。输入今天看到的价格，它给 1 到 5 星，并且每天自动记录新的报价。
  3. 我们看完后，给了一份 0.5 版的修改建议。

Projects

Buy Now, or Wait?Fall 2026

Solo project · Python (pandas), Next.js, GitHub Actions · JFK–LAX nonstop economy fares

  • Built a three-page fare reference for JFK–LAX nonstop one-way economy tickets that rates a price from 1 to 5 stars against same-platform history.
  • Automated daily collection of real fare quotes with GitHub Actions, and used 2022 Expedia quotes (public Kaggle dataset, CC BY 4.0) to chart how prices move before departure.
  • Stated the limits on the page: a rating needs at least 10 independent reference days, thin samples are flagged, and results are labeled as historical, not a forecast.

这一段突出了什么

  • 路线写得具体（JFK–LAX），一看就知道做的范围有多大。
  • 每天自动采集真实报价，说明这不是一次性的作业。
  • 主动写局限。说「这是历史数据，不是预测」，比夸大更让人信。
